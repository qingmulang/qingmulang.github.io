Puzzle Wonderland · Privacy Policy

Effective: November 16, 2026 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

"Puzzle Wonderland" (the "App") is provided by individual developer qingmulang. We take your privacy seriously. In short: the App does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. All data stays locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no account system, no network requests, no third-party SDKs, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. Specifically:

  • No registration: we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any identity information.
  • No network connectivity: the App works fully offline and never sends data to any server.
  • No tracking or ads: no advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking, no analytics services.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

To support resume-play and progress tracking, the App stores a small amount of data locally, which never leaves your device:

  • Puzzle progress: the current unfinished board, elapsed time, and remaining hints, so you can continue later.
  • Completion records: completed levels and best times per difficulty, used to show completion marks.
  • Preferences: sound on/off and whether the tutorial has been seen.

This data is saved using system-provided local storage. Uninstalling the App removes all of it.

3. Photos and Library

When you use "Make your own puzzle", the App uses the iOS system photo picker (PHPicker) to let you choose an image.

  • The App does not request full photo library access; you only see the system picker.
  • The chosen image is used only in memory during the current session, and is never uploaded, saved, or shared.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from any user, do not show ads to children, and do not track them. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA).

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates no third-party SDKs, ad platforms, social login, payment, or analytics services, and contains no external links or network content.

6. Permissions

The App requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notification permissions. Photo selection is handled by the system picker without extra library permission.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the App's functionality changes in a way that affects data handling, we will update this policy with a new effective date. Under the current version, we collect no data.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us at revenlvxin@gmail.com.

“拼图乐园”（以下简称“本应用”）由个人开发者 qingmulang 提供。我们深知隐私的重要性，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。简而言之：本应用不收集、不上传、不共享你的任何个人数据，全部数据仅保存在你的设备本地。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何个人信息。本应用没有账号体系、没有网络请求、没有第三方 SDK、没有广告、也没有数据分析或追踪。具体而言：

  • 无需注册登录：不收集姓名、邮箱、电话或任何身份信息。
  • 无网络连接：应用完全离线运行，不会向任何服务器发送数据。
  • 无追踪与广告：不使用任何广告标识符（IDFA）、不跨应用追踪、不接入分析服务。

2. 设备上保存的数据

为了提供断点续玩和进度记录功能，本应用会在你的设备本地保存少量数据，这些数据永远不会离开你的设备：

  • 拼图进度：当前未完成的拼图局面、已用时间与提示次数，用于你下次继续游戏。
  • 通关记录：已完成的关卡与各难度的最佳用时，用于在首页显示完成标记。
  • 偏好设置：音效开关、新手引导是否已读。

以上数据通过系统提供的本地存储机制保存。你卸载应用时，这些数据会随应用一并被系统删除。

当你选择“拼自己的照片”功能时，应用会使用 iOS 系统的照片选择器（PHPicker）让你挑选一张图片作为拼图素材。

  • 你选择的图片仅在当前游戏会话中于设备内存里使用，不会被上传、保存或发送给任何第三方。

4. 儿童隐私

本应用面向包括儿童在内的各年龄用户。我们不收集任何年龄用户的个人信息，也不会针对儿童投放广告或进行追踪。我们遵守适用的儿童隐私保护法规（如 COPPA、《儿童个人信息网络保护规定》等）。

5. 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方 SDK、广告平台、社交登录、支付或数据分析服务，也不包含任何外链或网络内容。

6. 权限说明

本应用不申请相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录、通知等敏感权限。选图功能由系统照片选择器提供，不额外索取相册权限。

7. 政策更新

若未来本应用的功能发生变化导致数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本政策并注明新的生效日期。当前版本下，我们不收集任何数据。

8. 联系我们
