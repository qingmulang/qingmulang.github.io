Puzzle Wonderland

A calming jigsaw game for the whole family. Offline. No ads. No sign-in.

About

Puzzle Wonderland is a relaxing jigsaw puzzle game with beautiful illustrations, gentle sounds, and satisfying snap mechanics. Pieces snap together when close enough, correctly joined pieces move as a group, and your progress is saved automatically.

Privacy

The app collects no data, requires no account, and works fully offline. Read the full Privacy Policy.

All puzzle progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
